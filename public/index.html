How ML works here

All three models run client-side, in the browser. The server never trains or runs a model.

  1. Recommender · cosine similarityItem-to-item collaborative filtering blended with product attribute vectors, computed in the buyer’s browser on the shop page.
  2. Fair-price MLP · TensorFlow.jsAn 8→32→16→2 network trained in the seller’s browser in Seller Studio. It predicts a fair price and weekly orders.
  3. Photo tagger · MobileNet CNNA pre-trained ImageNet model that tags the product photo in AI Catalogue. The photo stays on the device until the seller publishes it.
